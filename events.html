THE FORMAT

Event track · One Open class · Maximum 40 racers

  1. Sign up below with your rider name, number and UID.
  2. Join 317mx.duckdns.org:19800 and complete a clean flying lap on Event track. The opening lap from the gate is not a qualifying lap.
  3. Stay connected for the 11:30 PM EST UID grab. The fastest 40 signed-up riders present with a valid time make the roster.
  4. Run the lineup check / one-lap warmup, followed by Moto 1 and Moto 2: 15 minutes + 1 lap each.

After the checkered flag, riders have up to 60 seconds to finish. Sessions normally have a 30-second break. Cody B can pause a session, manage the lineup, or correct results.
